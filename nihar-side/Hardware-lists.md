# Hardware lists

1. ESP32 DevKit V1
The brain of the whole device. Has built-in WiFi and Bluetooth. Ask exactly for "ESP32 DevKit V1" — there are many ESP32 variants, this specific one is what all tutorials use and what our code will be written for.
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹350–450

2. MPU6050 Module (GY-521)
The motion sensor. Detects tilt angle. Will be the core of posture detection. Ask for "GY-521 MPU6050 module" — it comes as a small blue PCB.
Buy: 2 units (1 spare, they're cheap, don't risk it) | Expected: ₹80–120 each

3. OLED Display — 0.96 inch, I2C, SSD1306
The small screen that shows GOOD / SLOUCHING. Must be the I2C version — tell the shopkeeper "I2C wala chahiye, SPI nahi." The driver chip name is SSD1306, shopkeepers will know it.
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹150–250

4. Vibration Motor Module
The buzz alert. Ask for "vibration motor module Arduino compatible" — buy the module version, not a bare motor. The module version has all the extra circuitry built in, much simpler to use.
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹60–100

5. Tactile Push Button
The calibration button. Any standard small push button works. They usually come in packs.
Buy: 1 pack | Expected: ₹10–20

6. Breadboard — Half Size
Your prototyping board. Everything plugs into this without soldering. Any brand works.
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹60–100

7. Jumper Wires — Male to Male, pack of 40
The wires connecting everything on the breadboard. Ask for "jumper wire male to male 40 piece pack."
Buy: 1 pack | Expected: ₹50–80

8. LiPo Battery — 3.7V, 1000mAh
Portable power for the wearable. Ask for "lithium polymer battery 3.7 volt 1000mAh."
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹150–200

9. TP4056 Charging Module
Charges the LiPo safely via USB and feeds power to the circuit. Ask for "TP4056 lithium battery charging module." Tiny blue board.
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹50–80

10. Small Plastic Project Enclosure Box
Houses the whole device. Roughly matchbox to soap-bar size. Any generic hobby electronics box. Ask for "plastic project box" or "electronics enclosure box."
Buy: 1 unit | Expected: ₹50–80

11. Elastic or Velcro Strap
To wear the box on the upper back. A wide elastic sports band or velcro strip. Fabric/stationery shop if not available at electronics shops.
Buy: enough for a back strap | Expected: ₹30–60
